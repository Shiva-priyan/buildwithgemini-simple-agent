# My agent: FitFuel Coach & Wellness Assistant
One-liner: A conversational health and wellness agent that helps users achieve their fitness goals with a catalog of wholesome meals, recipes, and workout plans.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers user physical profile, dietary restrictions, target weight, and daily calorie targets across sessions.
- Tools: Firestore catalog lookup, logging meals, reading workout regimens, and burn-off duration calculations.
- Catalog/UI: Catalog of verified meals and exercises to render as informative cards and tables.
- Image gen: Generates visual representations of balanced plates and workout postures.
- Sandbox: MET energy expenditure calculations and Mifflin-St Jeor TDEE calibration.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Firestore database storage, code calculations, Cloud Trace
First eval question: What should I eat for a high-protein lunch around 500 kcal, and what are its macros?
